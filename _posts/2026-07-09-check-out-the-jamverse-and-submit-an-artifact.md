I'm cohosting an experimental project called the [Jamverse](https://jamverse.protocolized.io/). It's a quasi-shared worldbuilding space under the auspices of the Protocol Foundation. My contribution is the series of short stories I'm writing under the working title The Stockton Chronicles, set in a near future northern California. There are also series from three other authors: Elizabeth Maher, Sachin Benny, and Spencer Nitkey.

The experiment we're running this month is a contest where we invite others to create in-fiction artifacts set in one (or more) of our worlds. These are small artifacts like a product review, a flyer, or a press release. You can submit multiple artifacts and we're giving out $3,000 in prizes. You can read more about the contest and submit your own contribution here: [https://jamverse.protocolized.io/contest](https://jamverse.protocolized.io/contest)
